A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.

Who is CedarBayInc for?+

We work with businesses that need software shaped around their own needs—from a new digital product to a better way of running everyday operations. If a ready-made solution doesn't quite fit, we'd like to hear from you.

What happens after I join the waitlist?+

The form prepares an email to support@cedarbay.cc. Once you send it, we'll have your project introduction and contact details to discuss fit and availability. Joining does not reserve a start date or create a commitment.

Do I need a finished brief?+

Not at all. A problem you'd like to solve or an idea you'd like to explore is a good place to start. Share what you know, and we can work through the details together.

How are pricing and timelines decided?+

Every project is different. Scope, complexity, and priorities shape the estimate. We'll discuss these with you before agreeing on a budget, timeline, or next steps.
